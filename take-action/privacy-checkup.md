<header class="page-header"><p class="eyebrow">TAKE CONTROL</p><h1>Your 15-minute privacy checkup</h1><p>Choose one account or app. Work through the checklist at your own pace.</p></header>
<div class="prose"><h2>1. Secure the account</h2><ul><li>Use a unique password or passkey if supported.</li><li>Enable multi-factor authentication where available.</li><li>Check recovery email addresses, phone numbers and active sessions.</li></ul><h2>2. Review what you share</h2><ul><li>Check whether your profile and posts are public.</li><li>Review location sharing and tagging settings.</li><li>Remove connected apps you no longer use.</li></ul><h2>3. Review data and advertising controls</h2><ul><li>Look for activity history and ad-personalization controls.</li><li>Find the account's data download and deletion options.</li><li>Read the current privacy notice for sharing and retention details.</li></ul><div class="callout"><strong>Make one change today.</strong> A small, completed action is more useful than a long list you never get around to.</div></div>
