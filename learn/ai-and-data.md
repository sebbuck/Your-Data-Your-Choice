<header class="page-header"><p class="eyebrow">THE DIGITAL WORLD AHEAD · 7 MIN READ</p><h1>How does AI use data?</h1><p>Data can play different roles across the development and use of artificial intelligence systems.</p></header>
<article class="prose"><h2>Training and evaluation</h2><p>Some AI systems are trained or evaluated using large datasets. Data sources and safeguards vary by system. A dataset may contain personal information, but that is not true of every dataset or model.</p><h2>Prompts and uploaded content</h2><p>When you use an AI service, you may provide prompts, documents, images or other content. The service's terms and settings determine how that information is processed, retained or used to improve services.</p><h2>AI-generated inferences</h2><p>AI systems can classify, summarize, recommend or predict. Outputs can be inaccurate or biased, and automated decisions can affect people in consequential ways.</p><h2>Before using an AI tool</h2><ul><li>Do not enter sensitive personal information unless you understand the service and have a legitimate reason.</li><li>Review privacy, retention and model-improvement settings.</li><li>Check important outputs rather than assuming they are correct.</li><li>Ask whether a human can review a consequential decision.</li></ul></article>
