<header class="page-header"><p class="eyebrow">DIGITAL IDENTITY · 6 MIN READ</p><h1>What is your digital footprint?</h1><p>Your digital footprint is the trail of information associated with your activity across websites, apps, devices and connected services.</p></header>
<article class="prose">
<h2>Active and passive traces</h2><p>An active footprint includes information you deliberately share: posts, comments, photos, reviews, profile details and messages. A passive footprint can include information generated as you use services, such as device details, browsing activity, approximate location or how long you view something.</p>
<h2>Data can create inferences</h2><p>Individual signals may seem ordinary. When combined over time, they can help a service infer interests, routines, likely preferences or broad characteristics. An inference is a prediction, not necessarily a fact about you.</p>
<h2>Who might use it?</h2><p>Depending on the service and its practices, information may be used to provide features, personalize content, measure performance, prevent fraud, target advertising or support analytics. Some information may be shared with service providers or other organizations.</p>
<h2>What can you do?</h2><ul><li>Review app permissions, especially location, contacts, microphone and photos.</li><li>Check public profile settings and old connected applications.</li><li>Read the service's privacy information to understand collection, use, sharing and retention.</li><li>Use the <a href="{{ '/tools/digital-footprint/' | relative_url }}">Digital Footprint Explorer</a> to explore examples.</li></ul>
<div class="callout"><strong>Remember:</strong> There is no single universal digital footprint. What is collected depends on the service, your settings, your device and the law that applies.</div>
