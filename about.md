<header class="page-header"><p class="eyebrow">ABOUT THE PROJECT</p><h1>Digital rights should be understandable.</h1><p>Your Data, Your Call is an independent educational initiative focused on helping young Canadians make informed choices about personal information and digital technology.</p></header>
<div class="prose"><h2>Our mission</h2><p>To make digital rights understandable and actionable through clear explanations, practical guides and interactive learning tools.</p><h2>Who this is for</h2><p>Our primary audience is young people in Canada, while the resources are designed to be useful to anyone who wants to understand their digital life.</p><h2>Independence</h2><p>This project is independently developed. Educational content should not be shaped by advertisers or commercial sponsors. Any future funding, partnerships or conflicts of interest should be disclosed clearly.</p><h2>Get involved</h2><p>Suggestions, corrections and contributions can be submitted through this public GitHub repository.</p><p><a href="https://github.com/sebbuck/Your-Data-Your-Choice/issues">Suggest a correction or improvement →</a></p></div>
