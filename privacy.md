<header class="page-header"><p class="eyebrow">PRACTICE WHAT WE TEACH</p><h1>Our privacy approach.</h1><p>This project aims to collect as little information about visitors as possible.</p></header>
<div class="prose"><h2>Current site design</h2><ul><li>No visitor account is required.</li><li>The Digital Footprint Explorer runs locally in your browser and does not submit selections.</li><li>We do not intentionally add advertising trackers or behavioural profiling.</li><li>GitHub Pages and other hosting infrastructure may process standard technical logs under their own terms.</li></ul><h2>Future changes</h2><p>If analytics, forms, mailing lists, embedded media or other third-party services are introduced, this page must be updated to explain what data is collected, why, by whom and how visitors can make choices.</p><p class="source-note">This page describes the intended project design. Review the hosting provider's current privacy documentation before launch.</p></div>
