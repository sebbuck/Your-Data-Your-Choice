<header class="page-header"><p class="eyebrow">KNOW YOUR RIGHTS · CANADA</p><h1>Canadian privacy rights: where to start.</h1><p>Canada does not have one single privacy law that applies in exactly the same way to every organization and situation.</p></header>
<article class="prose"><h2>Why the details matter</h2><p>The applicable rules can depend on whether the organization is a federal institution, a private-sector business, a provincial or municipal public body, or a health-information custodian. Province, sector and information type can all matter.</p><h2>Federal private-sector privacy</h2><p>The Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) applies to many private-sector organizations in commercial activities, subject to important exceptions and substantially similar provincial laws.</p><h2>Provincial laws</h2><p>Alberta, British Columbia and Quebec have general private-sector privacy laws recognized as substantially similar to PIPEDA for relevant activities. Ontario has specific legislation governing personal health information. Public-sector and health privacy laws may also apply in other contexts.</p><h2>Questions to investigate</h2><ul><li>Can I ask an organization for access to my personal information?</li><li>Can I request a correction?</li><li>What consent rules apply?</li><li>How do I raise a concern or make a complaint?</li></ul><div class="callout"><strong>Important:</strong> This page is an educational starting point, not legal advice. A specific situation requires checking current law, regulator guidance and facts.</div><h2>Authoritative starting points</h2><ul><li><a href="https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/" rel="noopener">Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada: Privacy laws in Canada</a></li><li><a href="https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/" rel="noopener">Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada</a></li><li><a href="https://www.ipc.on.ca/" rel="noopener">Information and Privacy Commissioner of Ontario</a></li><li><a href="https://www.oipc.ab.ca/" rel="noopener">Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta</a></li><li><a href="https://www.oipc.bc.ca/" rel="noopener">Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for BC</a></li><li><a href="https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca/" rel="noopener">Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec</a></li></ul></article>
