How this site treats your data

A privacy guide should follow its own advice. As built, this site:

  • has no accounts, analytics, cookies or advertising;
  • loads no outside fonts, scripts or images, so your browser contacts only this site;
  • saves your plan only in your browser's local storage on this device, and "Start over" erases it.

Like any website, the company that hosts it can see basic connection details such as your IP address.
